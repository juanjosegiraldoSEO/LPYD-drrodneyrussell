The one thing that matters most

Three of every four requests to the homepage get a blank verification screen instead of the homepage. The Sucuri firewall in front of the site answers with an 11,863-byte “Please wait while your request is being verified…” page — no headline, no copy, no links, no structured data — and it caches that page and serves it as if it were content. Measured across 75 requests: 58 walled, 17 real.

Every other page on the site returns real HTML every time. It is the homepage alone — the page carrying 72% of the site’s clicks and 66% of all landing sessions, and the page that has to establish the clinic’s name, address, phone and specialty for local search.

Two other things sit alongside it. 310 WooCommerce tag URLs are submitted for indexing and Google refuses exactly 310 — a one-toggle fix. And nothing on the site is tracked as a conversion: zero key events, $0.00 revenue, while roughly 218 users a period reach a booking page. That last one cannot be fixed retroactively.

Everything else in this report is downstream of those three.
